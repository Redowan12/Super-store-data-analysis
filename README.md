# 🛒 Superstore Business Analysis
### Data Analysis Project — Pandas, NumPy & Matplotlib

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project performs a business analysis on the Sample Superstore dataset to investigate profitability patterns, the impact of discounting on profit, and seasonal sales trends. The analysis is conducted using Python libraries — Pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.

---

## 🎯 Research Questions
1. Which regions and product categories are the most and least profitable?
2. Does offering higher discounts reduce overall profit?
3. What seasonal trends exist in sales performance?

---

## 📂 Dataset
- **Name:** Sample Superstore Dataset
- **Source:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final)
- **Rows:** 9,994
- **Columns:** 21
- **Period:** 2014 – 2017

## 📊 Key Findings
- **West region** is the most profitable (13.98% margin); **Central** underperforms (7.32%)
- **Technology** is the most profitable category; **Furniture** suffers from low margins
- Higher discounts strongly correlate with profit loss (correlation = −0.219)
- **Q4 (Oct–Dec)** is consistently the best sales period across all years
- **18.7%** of all orders resulted in financial losses due to heavy discounting
